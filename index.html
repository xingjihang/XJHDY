Working with us

Four things that decide whether an order goes smoothly

Buyers do not lose money on the price of the equipment. They lose it on a nameplate that does not match the drawing, a test report that never arrives, or a container held at the port because the certificate of origin was wrong.

Factory price, not trading-company price

We are the export arm of the plant, not a middle layer. What you save is the margin, not the copper — winding material, core grade and breaker brand stay exactly as specified.

A quotation inside 12 hours, in writing

Not a "contact us for price" reply. A PDF with model, kVA, voltage ratio, vector group, enclosure rating, unit price, packing dimensions and lead time.

Every unit routine-tested before it ships

Ratio, insulation resistance, power-frequency withstand, no-load loss and no-load current. The signed report travels with the packing list, not in a follow-up email.

Export paperwork handled end to end

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin (CO or Form E), CE declaration, and fumigated crating for sea freight. We book the container if you want CIF or DDP.

Manufacturing & quality

One plant, one standard, no sub-contracted windings

Coils are wound, cast and cured in-house. Core lamination is stacked and clamped in-house. That matters because the two things that fail in the field — inter-turn insulation and core clamping pressure — are exactly the two things a sub-contractor will cut first.

Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

One unit for stabilizers and distribution transformers — we ship samples and single units by LCL all the time. Switchgear is quoted per project because the bay count and breaker schedule drive the price; a single bay is fine.

How quickly will I get a price?

Within 12 working hours for a standard configuration. Custom vector groups, non-standard voltages, or a tender with a bill of quantities may take up to 2 working days because the engineering team has to check the design.

What is the lead time?

15–25 days for stabilizers and dry-type transformers up to 1600 kVA; 25–35 days for larger units and switchgear assemblies. Sea freight to Jeddah, Dubai or Lagos adds roughly 18–30 days depending on the routing.

What payment terms do you accept?

30% T/T deposit with 70% before shipment as standard. L/C at sight for orders above US$50,000. For first orders into markets with currency controls we sometimes accept a higher deposit against a smaller balance — ask, it is negotiable.

Do you provide test reports and certificates?

Yes. Every unit ships with a routine test report covering ratio, insulation resistance, power-frequency withstand voltage, no-load loss and no-load current. Type test reports from a accredited third-party laboratory are available for standard models; for a specific project we can arrange SGS or Bureau Veritas pre-shipment inspection at cost.

Can you build to a non-standard voltage or frequency?

Yes — 50 Hz or 60 Hz, 220/380/400/415/440/480 V, 11/22/33/35 kV class, and any vector group (Dyn11, Yyn0, YNd11). For 60 Hz markets we adjust the core flux density rather than just relabelling a 50 Hz design, which is what keeps the losses and temperature rise correct.

Who handles shipping and customs?

You choose. FOB Shanghai / Ningbo / Qingdao if you have your own forwarder; CIF if you want us to book; DDP to most Middle East and African ports if you would rather not deal with clearance at all. We issue the commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and CE declaration either way.

What does the warranty cover?

36 months from the date of shipment against manufacturing defects, including winding failure under rated conditions. Consumables and damage from overload, lightning or incorrect installation are excluded. Replacement parts are shipped by air at our cost within the warranty period.
