Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens to the information you send us through this website. It applies to enquiries, quotation requests and email correspondence.

Who we are

Macheng Xingjihang Import & Export Co., Ltd., Macheng Economic Development Zone, Huanggang, Hubei 438300, China. We are the data controller for information submitted through this site. You can reach us at sales@example.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form or send us by email:

  • Your name and company name
  • Your email address and, if you provide it, your phone or WhatsApp number
  • The technical requirement you describe
  • Any files you send us afterwards, such as drawings or specifications

We also receive standard server log data from our hosting provider — IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamp — which is used for security and for keeping the site online.

Why we collect it

To answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation, and correspond with you about an order. That is the only purpose. We do not build marketing profiles, and we do not send newsletters unless you explicitly ask to be added to a list.

Who sees it

Our own sales staff, and — where a technical question requires it — the engineering team at the manufacturing plant that would build your equipment. We do not sell, rent or trade enquiry data to anyone.

Two service providers process data on our behalf: our email host and our form-handling provider (Formspree). Both act as processors under our instruction. Where a courier or freight forwarder needs your delivery address to ship an order, we pass on only what is needed to deliver.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an order are deleted after 24 months. Order records, invoices and shipping documents are kept for as long as tax and customs law requires, which in China is generally ten years.

Cookies

This website sets no tracking cookies of its own. If we later run advertising through Google or Meta, those platforms may set cookies to measure which advertisement brought you here; where that happens we will ask for consent first in the regions that require it, and you can refuse without losing access to any part of the site.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to sales@example.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to enquiry data is limited to staff who need it to answer you. We do not store payment card details — payments are made by bank transfer or letter of credit.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and change the date below. Material changes affecting existing enquiries will be notified by email.
